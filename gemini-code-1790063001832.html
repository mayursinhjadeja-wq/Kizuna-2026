Important Notes & Guidelines

Please DO NOT carry liquor while returning to Gujarat. Gujarat is a dry state with strict legal prohibition laws.

Alcohol is sponsored by the Company ONLY during dinner at Thalassa and KIKI – By the Sea. Any outside consumption will be self-sponsored.
